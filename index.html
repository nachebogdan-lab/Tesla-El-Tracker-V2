Import charging history (CSV)

From the Tesla app export (columns: start time, duration in minutes, kWh). Where you already have real hourly numbers (from a screenshot day), they're used to work out how much of that consumption was the car — otherwise the session is split evenly across its duration.

Import house consumption (CSV)

From a Min Strøm / Go Energi export (columns: Date, Consumption (kWh), Total price (kr/kWh), ...). Fills in kWh and price for every hour automatically — and recalculates the car-charging split if you've already imported charging history.

Import solar export (CSV)

Hourly kWh sold to the grid (columns: Date, Export (kWh)). This is energy your panels produced but the house didn't use — shown as its own column, valued at that hour's price as an estimate of what it was worth.
